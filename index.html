ABOUT THE GAME

A plant is not a checklist.
It is a living system.

Grow a plant by observing its signals and shaping its environment. There is no instant “correct answer”: your choices settle gradually, and the plant responds over time.

01

YOUR GOAL

Keep the system in balance and grow four healthy leaves within seven days.

02

HOW TO PLAY

Adjust water, light, temperature, or nutrients when the plant needs it. You may also leave everything alone.

03

LET TIME PASS

Press LET ONE DAY PASS. The environment and plant change gradually, not instantly.

04

READ THE SIGNALS

Use the leaves, roots, soil, health, and growth rate to infer what is happening.
